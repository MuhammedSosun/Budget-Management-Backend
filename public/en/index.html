Features

A financial dashboard that does more than record transactions

Budgetly goes beyond simple income and expense tracking. With workspaces, member-based spending insights, budget limits, notifications, and AI-powered reviews, it helps you understand your financial activity more clearly.

AI-Powered Monthly Review

Gemini integration summarizes your monthly financial situation, highlights risks, and provides practical suggestions based on your spending patterns.

Workspace-Based Budget Management

Create separate spaces for personal or shared budgets. Manage family, team, or category-based spending areas with a clean workspace structure.

Member Spending Analysis

See how spending is distributed across workspace members. Understand who spent the most and how much each member contributed to total expenses.

Budget Limits

Set monthly limits for categories such as groceries, transportation, food, or custom spending areas to keep your budget easier to control.

Smart Notification Structure

Budgetly is designed to notify you around important financial activity and budget limit status, helping you notice critical situations at the right time.

Multilingual Support and Dark Mode

Use Budgetly in Turkish or English, with a modern dark mode experience that adapts to different usage preferences.

AI Review

Budgetly does not just show numbers, it turns them into insight

Budgetly does not send raw transaction data directly to the AI model. The backend first prepares safe financial metrics, then AI converts those metrics into a clear, user-friendly report. This makes the analysis more controlled, understandable, and reliable.

Category Concentration

Understand which category dominates your total expenses with clear percentage-based insights.

Member Spending Share

Risks and Suggestions
